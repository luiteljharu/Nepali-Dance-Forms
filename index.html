01 / Why dance?

More than performance.

In Nepal, dance is woven into festivals, harvests, worship, courtship, storytelling, and community life.

Nepal's dance heritage is not one single tradition. It is a living collection of cultural practices shaped by different communities, regions, languages and histories.

Some dances are ceremonial. Some are performed during festivals. Others preserve stories, celebrate harvests, strengthen community ties or pass traditions from one generation to the next.
